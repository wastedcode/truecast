# Working Backwards PR/FAQ

**Why it's gold**
- Forces the customer's view first: a one-sentence heading and subheading, then the problem "from the
  customer's point of view", then how this differs from current alternatives.
- Splits FAQs into **external** (customers, press) and **internal** (leaders and every department: technical,
  financial, legal, operational problems to solve).
- "Designed to be lightweight": a first draft should take a few hours.

**Weakness**
- Built for new products; overkill for a feature on an existing surface (use pm-007 or pm-051).

**Use when** the idea is a new product or a bet that needs a yes from leadership.
