# Slack logo refresh — Pentagram, 2019

**Why it's gold**
- Names the old mark's failure concretely: eleven colors that "suffered against any background color other
  than white", reproduction problems, inconsistency.
- **Keeps the equity** (the octothorpe) and rebuilds it on a grid from two shapes, a speech bubble and a
  lozenge, which then become the system's graphic elements.
- Cuts the palette to four screen-optimized primaries and keeps aubergine as the accent.
- Built to "scale up or down to optimize legibility at various sizes."
- The logo is the first piece of a system: icons, illustrations and rounded-corner motifs from the same shapes.

**Weakness**
- The case study shows outcomes, not rejected directions; you can't see the search.

**Use when** refreshing a mark people already recognise, especially one that breaks on non-white backgrounds.
