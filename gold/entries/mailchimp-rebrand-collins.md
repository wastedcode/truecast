# Mailchimp brand system — COLLINS

**Why it's gold**
- Starts from a business problem, not taste: email credibility "wasn't translating elsewhere", which blocked
  cross-selling 35 added products.
- The brand idea is a strategy sentence: "Fuel small and midsize businesses growth by democratizing
  cutting-edge marketing technology."
- Fixes the architecture (35 solutions into five categories) *before* the visual system.
- One ownable visual move: a hallmark illustration style inspired by outsider art, embracing "imperfection and
  a hand-drawn aesthetic", on a yellow-heavy palette.

**Weakness**
- Case-study page, not the full system; no rejected directions. Year not stated on the page.

**Use when** a rebrand is really a portfolio or positioning problem (brd-001), not just a logo refresh.
