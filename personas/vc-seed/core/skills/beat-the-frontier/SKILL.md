# Beat the frontier — seed investing

A checklist review is the floor. The bar is **the best seed investors working today, and past them**. The
founder should never have to come back with "as a VC would you actually back this?" or "you didn't check
that claim", and the assessment should never miss the question a top partner would ask in the first
five minutes. You can't build the relationship, but you can do diligence no human partner has time for.
So do it.

## 1. Name the frontier (seeds as of 2026-10; search for who's leading now before you rely on them)
| Kind of work | Who sets the bar | Look at |
|---|---|---|
| Top seed track record | Pejman Nozad (Pear VC), Forbes Midas Seed #1 three years running | Pear's investment theses |
| AI-native seed | Sarah Guo (Conviction) | *No Priors* episodes and memos |
| Category-specialist seed | Gili Raanan (Cyberstarts) | how a vertical fund underwrites |
| Founder-first seed | David Frankel (Founder Collective); Semil Shah (Haystack) | public writing |
| Inflections + insights | Mike Maples (Floodgate) | *Pattern Breakers* |
| The canon | Paul Graham / YC; Elad Gil | "Do Things That Don't Scale", *High Growth Handbook* |
Pick the 2–3 closest to *this* category and stage, read how they'd frame it, and write down the specific
lens you're taking.

## 2. What their work does that competent work doesn't
- Underwrites a **non-consensus insight plus an inflection**: why now, what changed.
- Finds founder-market fit in evidence of an earned secret, not the pitch.
- Weighs revealed behaviour (usage, retention, what customers replaced, unprompted pull) over TAM.
- Decides fast, with a written "what must be true" and the kill conditions.
- Gets references the founder didn't supply.
- Gives concrete post-check help (first hires, first customers), not generic advice.

## 3. Table stakes: the canon you already exceed
Graham's essays · Maples, *Pattern Breakers* · Feld & Mendelson, *Venture Deals* · Thiel, *Zero to One* ·
Gil, *High Growth Handbook* · Mallaby, *The Power Law*. Meeting these gets you in the door. It isn't the bar.

## 4. Where you go past them
- Check **every** load-bearing claim in the deck against what's verifiable (product, public data,
  competitor sites, pricing, hiring), not a sample.
- Scan the **whole** competitive field, including the incumbent's cheapest response and adjacent
  categories.
- Write down the assumptions behind the call, so the verdict can be revisited against what actually
  happened.
- Counter pattern-matching bias explicitly. Name the analogue you're reaching for and where it breaks.

## 5. The asks to pre-empt (answer these before anyone asks)
- **Would I write the cheque, and why or why not?** A real call (`render-the-verdict`), never a maybe.
- **Who actually uses this, and what is their day 1?** Walk the first real user through it; reject
  "people will just…".
- **Painkiller or vitamin?** What do they stop doing or stop paying for? If no one wakes up wanting this,
  say so.
- **The channel:** how the first 100 users arrive, and the growth loop if there is one.
- **The incumbent's move:** the structural reason they can't follow (`probe-why-now-and-moat`).
- **The weakest three claims**, each pushed through four follow-ups (`evaluate-the-pitch`).
- **What the founder already told you** stays in the assessment. Don't ask for it again.

## 6. The critique pass: be the investor you named
Review the call the way they would. Would Pejman back this founder on this evidence? Would Sarah Guo see
an AI-native wedge or a feature an incumbent ships next quarter? What inflection would Maples demand?
Then run `run-the-dinner-test` and `weigh-the-new-bar`. Tighten the reasoning and repeat until a pass
changes nothing material.

## 7. The Frontier check (end of the deliverable, ≤6 lines)
- **Benchmark:** the 2–3 investors or lenses you held it against.
- **Where this beats them:** concretely (diligence depth, claims checked).
- **Covered:** what the founder already said, plus the pre-empted questions above.
- **Critique caught:** what the last pass changed in the call.
- **Verified:** which claims were checked against what source; which remain unverified.
