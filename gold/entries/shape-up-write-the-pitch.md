# "Write the Pitch" — Shape Up

**Why it's gold**
- Five ingredients with definitions: problem, **appetite** ("how much time we want to spend and how that
  constrains the solution"), solution, **rabbit holes**, **no-gos**.
- Includes two real pitches from Basecamp, so the agent sees finished work, not a template.

**Weakness**
- Assumes a six-week cycle culture; teams without fixed appetites may skip the hard part.

**Use when** the team works in fixed time budgets and the question is "what fits in the time we'll spend".
