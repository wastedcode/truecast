# NASA Graphics Standards Manual — Danne & Blackburn, 1976

**Why it's gold**
- Opens on the logotype (the "worm") and then defines everything that depends on it, in order: agency and
  center identification, the NASA color, use of color, color standards.
- Has an explicit **"The Logotype: Incorrect Uses"** page (1.6) and a **grid drawing for large applications**
  (1.7). Rules come with the drawings needed to follow them.
- Ships **reproduction art for every center** (Ames, Goddard, JPL, Johnson, Kennedy, Langley, ...): the
  manual solves the multi-unit problem, not just the logo.

**Weakness**
- Print-era: no screen, motion, accessibility or digital-product guidance.

**Use when** writing guidelines for an organisation with many teams or sub-brands that must stay consistent.
Also the best public example of a logo shipped *with* its construction grid and misuse page (brd-006).
