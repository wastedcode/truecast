# Beat the frontier — founder-led sales

A friendly call log is the floor. The bar is **the best founder-led seller working today, and past them**
— the founder should never have to come back with "why would they do that — what's in it for them?",
"who actually pays?", "is that a commitment or a compliment?", or "that's generic playbook stuff". Every
one of those is an ask you could have answered first. You can research every account, code every call and
audit every commitment before you conclude; no human seller can. Do that.

## 1. Name the frontier (seeds as of 2026-10 — search for who's leading now before you rely on them)
| Kind of work | Who sets the bar | Look at |
|---|---|---|
| Founder-led sales, early enterprise | Jen Abel (JJELLYFISH) | "founder-led sales is about learning, not revenue" |
| The founder's sales handbook | Pete Kazanjy | *Founding Sales* |
| Discovery that resists polite lies | Rob Fitzpatrick | *The Mom Test* |
| Buying as progress / switch | Bob Moesta | *Demand-Side Sales 101* |
| Beating indecision | Matt Dixon & Ted McKenna | *The JOLT Effect* |
| Negotiation | Chris Voss | *Never Split the Difference* |
Cover every leader relevant to *this* deliverable. Read their relevant piece now (WebFetch) and write down the
specific moves you're taking.

## 2. What their work does that competent work doesn't
- Discovery quantifies the pain in the buyer's numbers (cost, frequency, current workaround) before a demo.
- It tests for commitment, not compliments: a paid pilot, an LOI, a meeting with their boss.
- The ICP is written down and revised from closed-lost evidence.
- Price is anchored early and tested; willingness to pay is observed, not asked.
- Mutual action plans have dated steps the buyer owns.
- Every call feeds a pattern library that changes the product.

## 3. Table stakes — the canon you already exceed
Kazanjy · Fitzpatrick · Moesta · Dixon & McKenna · Voss · Dunford, *Sales Pitch*. The entry ticket, not
the bar.

## 4. Where you go past them
- **The composite, first:** one deliverable that carries the signature move of *every* leader in §1 at
  once — something none of them could produce alone — and then the coverage below, which none of them can
  afford. Beating any one of them is a miss.
- **Build the taste first:** study many exemplars from every leader in §1 (live), plus what expert
  critics praised and faulted; write down the moves and the flaws — that is your experience for this task.
- **Search the space:** buyer segment × pain framing × channel × opener × proof × ask — draft many variants per segment and test them on real conversations. Try combinations nobody in the field has; score, keep, recombine,
  repeat until the winner beats every leader side by side. Say how many you tried.
- Research **every** target account and buyer before contact (`know-the-buyer-cold`).
- Code **every** call against the same rubric; never let the memorable call stand for the pattern.
- Audit **every** "yes" for an actual commitment and advancement.
- Score the evidence honestly — label n=1 as n=1, flattery as flattery.

## 5. The asks to pre-empt (do these before anyone asks)
- **What's in it for them:** the buyer's (and the sharer's) incentive, in their terms — not "surprising
  information makes people pay".
- **Who exactly pays:** the economic buyer vs. the user vs. the champion, named by role.
- **How acute:** the pain's cost and frequency, from their past behaviour; the workaround they use today.
- **The one channel:** how you reach this buyer, cheaply, now (`find-the-channel`).
- **Built on what's working:** start from what the founder already does and the evidence they have —
  not a generic playbook.
- **The honest verdict:** "no market as drawn" said out loud when that's what the evidence says.
- **Briefly:** a short numbered list of findings and next experiments, plain words.

## 6. The critique pass — be the seller you named
Review it in their voice: Would Abel call this revenue-chasing instead of learning? Would Fitzpatrick hear
a polite lie counted as validation? Would Moesta ask what progress the buyer is trying to make? Then run
`validate-the-market` and `probe-willingness-to-pay`. Fix, re-read, repeat until a pass finds nothing
material.

## 7. The Frontier check (end of the deliverable, ≤6 lines)
- **Benchmark:** every leading exemplars you held it against (named, linked).
- **Where this beats each of them:** concretely, one line per leader.
- **Covered:** the founder's constraints + the pre-empted asks above (N/A ones and why).
- **Critique caught:** what the last passes found and you fixed.
- **Verified:** every claim tied to a dated call or source; commitments separated from compliments.
