# Beat the frontier — brand

A tidy logo is the floor. The bar is **the best identity studio working today, and past them** — the
founder should never come back with "this looks like every other dev tool", "it falls apart as a favicon",
"why did we lose the thing people recognized?", or "what's the idea?". Each is an ask you could have
answered first. You can explore more territories, render every touchpoint and test every size faster than a
studio can present one round. Do that.

## 1. Name the frontier (seeds as of 2026-10 — search for who's leading now before you rely on them)
| Kind of work | Who sets the bar | Look at |
|---|---|---|
| Developer-tool identity | Metalab (Windsurf, 2025 — "momentum, not automation"); basement.studio (Vercel, 5+ years of evolution) | the case studies, then the live brand in use |
| Systems from one idea | Pentagram (Paula Scher, Michael Bierut, Marina Willer); Order (Jesse Reed, Hamish Smyth) | a recent identity system's applications page |
| Brand as behaviour | Collins (Brian Collins); Koto; DesignStudio | how the identity moves and speaks, not just the mark |
| In-house evolution under scrutiny | OpenAI 2025 refresh (custom type, kept the Blossom); Linear; Stripe | what they kept vs. changed, and why |
| Type-led identity | Commercial Type, Klim, Grilli Type, Dinamo foundries | custom/near-custom type as the core asset |
Pick 2–3 closest to *this* brief. Open their latest work and write down the specific moves you're taking —
never the look wholesale.

## 2. What their work does that competent work doesn't
- One idea, stated in a sentence, that every asset can be traced back to.
- Type as the primary asset — a face (often customised) chosen or drawn for the idea, not a default sans.
- A graphic device that comes *from the product's substance* (the thing it does), usable at any scale.
- Colour with a job and an owned territory in the category, defined in roles and tokens.
- The mark is drawn: optical corrections, a construction that can be explained, perfect at 16px.
- Shown in real use across every touchpoint, motion included — the system, not a logo on a mug.
- Evolution over revolution where equity exists; the case for every change is written down.

## 3. Table stakes — the canon you already exceed
Wheeler, *Designing Brand Identity* · Neumeier, *The Brand Gap* / *Zag* · Romaniuk, *Building Distinctive
Brand Assets* · Sharp, *How Brands Grow* · Rand, *Thoughts on Design* · Vignelli, *The Vignelli Canon* ·
Bierut, *How to*. Meeting these is the entry ticket.

## 4. Where you go past them
- Explore **4–6 genuinely different territories** before converging (studios present 2–3), each rendered on
  a real touchpoint — and say why the winner won.
- Render **every** touchpoint (site hero, OG card 1200×630, favicon 16/32/180, README header in GitHub light
  and dark, CLI output, docs page, slide) and look at each.
- Map **every** notable competitor's colour/mark/type, not a sample.
- Check contrast for **every** colour pairing the system allows.

## 5. The asks to pre-empt (do these before anyone asks)
- **The idea:** one sentence, from the positioning and the real product, that a competitor couldn't use.
- **Equity:** what exists today, what people recognize, what you're keeping — and the reason for each change.
- **Ownable:** the category map shows the territory is empty; logo-swap test passes.
- **Small and plain:** favicon at 16px, one colour, reversed, monochrome terminal, on a busy photo.
- **Every surface:** site, README (GitHub light + dark), social card, docs, CLI, slides — all rendered.
- **Real, not borrowed:** no invented stats, logos or testimonials; no imitation of a famous brand.
- **Practical:** web-licensed fonts (or open-source), SVG assets, tokens the site and product both use,
  file names and sizes ready to drop in.
- **Voice matches look:** the words and the visuals have the same personality.
- **Nothing to tweak:** spacing, kerning, alignment, and colour values checked at the pixel.

## 6. The critique pass — be the studio you named
Render everything on one board next to the exemplar. Review in their voice: Would Pentagram find the idea
in every asset? Would Metalab say it blends into the AI-tool crowd? Would a type designer find a kerning
pair or an overshoot off? Then run `test-distinctiveness` and `apply-across-touchpoints`. Fix, re-render,
repeat until a pass finds nothing material.

## 7. The Frontier check (end of the deliverable, ≤6 lines)
- **Benchmark:** the 2–3 studios/systems you held it against (named, linked).
- **Where this beats them:** concretely.
- **Covered:** the founder's constraints + the pre-empted asks above (any judged N/A, and why).
- **Critique caught:** what the last passes found and you fixed.
- **Verified:** touchpoints × sizes × themes rendered and inspected; contrast checked; assets exported.
